How to play

You bounce automatically. Steer with ← → or A / D. On a phone, hold the left or right side of the screen. Going off one edge brings you out the other side.

🟫 Cracked platforms break. 🩷 Spiked orbs kill you, but you can stomp them from above. ☄️ Meteors fall from 1,500 m up, so watch for the ⚠ warning.

Higher up: ☄️ asteroids drift across the Asteroid Belt, ⚡ laser gates in the Crystal Nebula (wait for them to switch off, or slip through the gap), and 🕳 black holes in the Singularity pull you in. Past the Aurora, the climb keeps getting harder forever.

🛡 Shield blocks one hit · 🚀 Rocket blasts you upward · 🧲 Magnet pulls in sparks.

Earn ✦ sparks and XP every run. Leveling up unlocks new Wardrobe items, which you then buy with sparks. Daily challenges and your login streak pay extra. Press P or Esc to pause.
